PLAYER PROFILE / 02

About me.

I’m Thomas Born, a game developer who enjoys making games.

I’ve wanted to become a game programmer since I was young. While playing games, I started wondering what it would be like to create one myself. I began experimenting with Scratch, and my parents bought me a book to help me learn programming. That early interest grew into the goal I still have today.

My Development Skills

I want to become a programmer who is proficient in multiple programming languages, giving me more options when solving programming challenges. I’ve worked on a tower defense game and gained experience with C#, Unity, basic game design, user interface implementation, and damage systems.

Outside of Game Development

I’ve also participated in sports such as tennis and soccer. I spent the most time playing soccer, where I played both as a goalkeeper and as an outfield player.

How I Work

I usually work independently but i have worked with a team.I can work best when I have a clear and concrete to-do list to follow. When I’m working on something I enjoy and can fully focus on, I perform at my best.

What kind of developer do I want to be?

My focus is gameplay programming and Combat VFX design. I’m looking for an internship where I can learn from experienced developers, apply feedback, and contribute to playable prototypes and complete game features.

What do I bring to the team?

Curiosity, persistence. I welcome feedback and want to make my code increasingly readable and maintainable.
